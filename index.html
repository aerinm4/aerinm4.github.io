Behind the lens

ABOUT ME

Music has always been a source of comfort and connection for me. I genuinely believe there is a song for every situation, and I take pride in always finding the right one. What started as a single concert in high school has grown into a passion that has taken me to 10 concerts so far. I’m a firm believer in gifting experiences over things, and I look forward to continuing to explore new artists, make memories, and share the joy of live music.
